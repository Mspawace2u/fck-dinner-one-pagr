## Layout and motion cues

- Large, airy pink hero with left-aligned brand-red typography
- Small italic kicker above huge stacked title
- Lower-page supporting paragraph and direct social links
- Rounded CTA pill treatment; high contrast and minimal ornament
- Avoid dark GlassHaus aesthetic in this project: inherit mechanics, not Patty's skin
- Mobile-first and accessible; preserve text contrast, button tap targets, reduced-motion behavior
